BNB (Binance Coin) is the native utility, gas, and governance token of the BNB Chain ecosystem, originally launched by Binance in 2017 as an ERC-20 token before migrating to its native blockchains.

The BNB Chain architecture comprises BNB Smart Chain (BSC), an Ethereum Virtual Machine (EVM) compatible smart contract platform, and BNB Greenfield, a decentralized storage network. BSC operates alongside the original BNB Beacon Chain (which is being merged into BSC).

BSC achieves consensus using Proof-of-Staked Authority (PoSA), combining Proof-of-Stake with Proof-of-Authority. A set of 21 to 45 active validators stake BNB and validate transactions, delivering fast block times (around 3 seconds) and low transaction fees.

BNB serves multiple functions: paying transaction fees (gas) on BSC, participating in network staking and governance, delegating to validators, and serving as a settlement medium within decentralized finance (DeFi) protocols and ecosystem applications.

BNB has an initial total supply cap of 200 million BNB, designed to reduce over time through an automated quarterly burn mechanism (BNB Auto-Burn) and real-time BEP-95 gas burning until the circulating supply reaches 100 million BNB.

Transactions require BNB to cover network gas fees. BNB wallets utilize standard cryptographic key pairs (BEP-20 address format compatible with Ethereum addresses).

Key risks and limitations include validator set centralization compared to larger permissionless networks, regulatory scrutiny surrounding its historical association with Binance, and potential network congestion during high transaction volume periods.
