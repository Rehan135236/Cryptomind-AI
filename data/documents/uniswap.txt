Source: Uniswap Protocol Whitepapers (v1, v2, v3, v4) by Hayden Adams et al. and Official Uniswap Documentation.

Uniswap is a leading decentralized exchange (DEX) protocol built on the Ethereum blockchain, created in November 2018 by Hayden Adams.

Uniswap pioneered the Automated Market Maker (AMM) model for cryptocurrency trading. Instead of traditional central limit order books, Uniswap relies on liquidity pools funded by liquidity providers (LPs).

Key protocol versions and innovations include:
1. Uniswap v1 / v2: Introduced ERC-20 to ERC-20 trading pools governed by the constant product automated market maker formula: x * y = k (where x and y represent asset reserves and k is a constant).
2. Uniswap v3: Introduced Concentrated Liquidity, allowing LPs to allocate capital within customized price ranges, dramatically increasing capital efficiency and reducing trade slippage.
3. Uniswap v4: Introduced a singleton contract architecture and custom plugin "Hooks" enabling dynamic fees, on-chain limit orders, and custom LP strategies.

UNI is the native ERC-20 governance token of the Uniswap protocol, launched in September 2020. UNI holders govern the Uniswap DAO treasury, vote on protocol fee switches, approve grant allocations, and manage cross-chain deployments.

UNI has a fixed initial total supply cap of 1,000,000,000 UNI tokens distributed over 4 years to community members, team members, investors, and advisors. A 2% annual inflation rate begins after year 4 to ensure ongoing participation incentives.

Trading fees are collected in target tokens and distributed directly to active liquidity providers. Swapping tokens on Uniswap requires Ethereum network gas fees paid in ETH.

Key risks and limitations include impermanent loss risk for liquidity providers during volatile price divergence, smart contract exploit risks, and regulatory scrutiny regarding decentralized exchange interfaces.
